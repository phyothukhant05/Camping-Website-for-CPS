CPS Campfield

Our flagship outdoor adventure destination and training facility

Adventure Awaits at Campfield

CPS Campfield is our premier outdoor adventure destination, offering a unique blend of camping experiences, outdoor training programs, and community events. Located in the heart of nature, Campfield serves as both a showcase for our products and a training ground for outdoor enthusiasts.

What We Offer

  • Guided camping trips and outdoor adventures
  • Product testing and demonstration events
  • Outdoor skills training workshops
  • Family-friendly camping experiences
  • Corporate team building programs

Visit Us

Experience the CPS difference firsthand at our Campfield location. Test our latest gear, join expert-led workshops, and connect with fellow outdoor enthusiasts in a beautiful natural setting.
