Shipping Policy

How we get your camping gear to you safely and efficiently.

Shipping Methods

We offer multiple shipping options to meet your needs:

  • Standard Shipping: 5-7 business days - $9.95
  • Express Shipping: 2-3 business days - $19.95
  • Overnight Shipping: 1 business day - $29.95
  • Free Shipping: On orders over $150 (Standard only)

Processing Time

Orders are typically processed within 1-2 business days. During peak seasons (May-August), processing may take up to 3 business days.

International Shipping

We currently ship within the United States and Canada. International shipping rates and delivery times vary by location.

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can track your package on our website or the carrier's website.

Returns & Refunds

Our hassle-free return policy ensures you're satisfied with your purchase.

Return Period

You have 30 days from the date of delivery to return items for a full refund or exchange.

Return Conditions

Items must be:

  • Unused and in original condition
  • In original packaging with all tags attached
  • Accompanied by proof of purchase

How to Return

1. Contact our customer service to initiate a return
2. Receive a prepaid shipping label via email
3. Package items securely and attach the label
4. Drop off at any authorized shipping location

Refund Process

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive your returned items. The refund will be issued to your original payment method.

Exchanges

Exchanges are processed immediately upon receipt of returned items. If the new item costs more, you'll pay the difference; if less, you'll receive a refund for the difference.

Product Care

Keep your camping gear in top condition with these care instructions.

Tents & Shelters

  • Clean with mild soap and water
  • Never machine wash or dry
  • Air dry completely before storage
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight

Sleeping Bags

  • Spot clean with damp cloth
  • Professional cleaning recommended for deep cleaning
  • Store uncompressed in a large storage bag
  • Avoid compression for extended periods

Camping Furniture

  • Wipe with damp cloth and mild detergent
  • Dry thoroughly to prevent rust
  • Lubricate moving parts annually
  • Store indoors when not in use

Lighting & Electronics

  • Remove batteries during storage
  • Clean with dry cloth only
  • Keep away from moisture
  • Check seals and gaskets regularly

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about CPS products and services.

How do I choose the right tent size?

Consider the number of people and gear you'll have. A 2-person tent typically fits 1-2 people comfortably with gear. Always choose one size larger if you're unsure.

Are your products waterproof?

Yes, all our tents and shelters feature waterproof materials with sealed seams. Look for the waterproof rating (mm) in product specifications.

Do you offer group discounts?

Yes! We offer 10% off for orders of 5+ items and 15% off for orders of 10+ items. Contact our sales team for bulk orders.

What's your best sleeping bag for cold weather?

Our Arctic Explorer series is rated for -20°F (-29°C) and features premium down insulation. Check the temperature rating on each product.

How do I clean my camping gear?

Refer to our Product Care section above for detailed cleaning instructions by product type.
